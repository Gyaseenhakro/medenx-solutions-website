Accessibility Statement

Last updated: August 22, 2026

Our Commitment

MeDenX Solutions is committed to making this website accessible to the widest possible audience, including people with disabilities. We aim to align with the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA.

Ongoing Efforts

  • Structuring content with clear headings and readable text hierarchy.
  • Maintaining color contrast that supports readability.
  • Supporting keyboard navigation across interactive elements.
  • Providing descriptive alternative text for meaningful images.

Feedback

If you encounter an accessibility barrier on this website, please contact us at info@medenxsolutions.com so we can address it.
