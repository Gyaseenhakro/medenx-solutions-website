Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2, 2026

Overview

MeDenX Solutions ("MeDenX," "we," "us") respects the privacy of visitors to this website. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how it is used, and the choices available to you.

Information We Collect

  • Information you provide directly through the Contact form: name, job title, organization, business email, phone number, organization type, service of interest, estimated monthly volume and your message.
  • Standard technical information recorded when a page is requested, such as IP address, browser type and referring page.

This website does not use advertising cookies or third-party marketing trackers, and does not currently run an analytics product.

How We Use Information

  • To respond to consultation requests and inquiries.
  • To communicate about the services you have inquired about.

Third-Party Services Used by This Website

This website relies on the third parties below. Each receives limited information when you use the site and operates under its own privacy policy:

  • Formspree — processes and delivers Contact form submissions. Everything entered in the form is transmitted to and stored by Formspree in order to deliver it to us.
  • GitHub Pages — hosts and serves this website, and receives the technical request information described above.
  • Google Fonts — serves the typefaces used on this site. Your browser requests these files from Google, which receives your IP address as part of that request.

Protected Health Information

This website does not knowingly collect Protected Health Information (PHI). Do not submit patient health information through this website's contact forms. PHI handled in the course of MeDenX's client services is governed separately under applicable Business Associate Agreements and HIPAA requirements.

Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information may be shared with service providers who support our website and business operations, subject to confidentiality obligations.

Your Choices

You may contact us at info@medenxsolutions.com to ask questions about this policy or request that we delete information you have submitted through this website.

Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy can be directed to info@medenxsolutions.com.
