Build a Career in Healthcare Operations

MeDenX supports the administrative, revenue-cycle, clinical-documentation and technology workflows that healthcare organizations depend on every day. If you want work where accuracy genuinely matters, this is that kind of work.

Why MeDenX

Work that healthcare organizations actually rely on.

Behind every claim, authorization and patient record is someone who has to get it right. These are the conditions we build that work around.

Work with real consequences

A denied claim, a delayed authorization or a missed recall has a direct effect on a practice and its patients. The work is operational, but it is not abstract.

Clinical and operational expertise together

Clinically qualified professionals work alongside our operations teams, so the people designing a workflow understand the clinical context behind it.

Operations and technology in one place

We run the workflow and build the technology behind it. That means operational roles sit close to the software and infrastructure teams, not separated from them.

How We Work

Structured, measured and reviewed — not improvised.

Every engagement runs on the same five-step approach: understand the workflow, optimize it, execute it, measure it against defined standards, then improve it. That applies to how we work internally too.

Documented workflows

You are not left to guess how something should be done. Processes are written down, owned and reviewed.

Measurable standards

Performance is tracked against defined operational standards rather than impressions, so good work is visible.

Safeguards taken seriously

Our HIPAA security safeguards have been reviewed by an independent third-party assessor. Everyone handling health information is expected to understand why that matters.

What Happens After You Apply

  • We confirm receipt of your application by email.
  • Your application is reviewed against the requirements for the role.
  • If there is a fit, we arrange an introductory conversation.
  • If there is not, we will tell you — and keep your details on file for future openings.

Before You Send

  • Check that your CV link is viewable by anyone with the link.
  • Name the role you are applying for — it routes your application faster.
  • Applying for more than one role? Send a separate application for each.
